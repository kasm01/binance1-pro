# SHORT Research Policy

- SHORT strategy pipeline is pre-refactor and must not be treated as production-validated.
- Continue collecting and analyzing SHORT market-context, microstructure, EMA, entry-confirmation and execution data.
- SHORT execution and queue research are strategy-independent enough to remain in the normal promotion research flow.
- SHORT strategy-derived results are labeled RESEARCH_ONLY / SHORT_REFACTOR_REQUIRED.
- Mixed LONG+SHORT micro-context results are labeled MIXED_RESEARCH_ONLY until analyzers are split by side.
- No SHORT strategy result may become CANDIDATE or PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE before the SHORT architecture refactor and replay/revalidation are complete.
